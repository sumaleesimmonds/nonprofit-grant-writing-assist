# Impact Statement

## The Problem This Solves

Grant writing is one of the most time-consuming, high-stakes tasks a nonprofit
faces. For large organizations with full-time development staff, it is manageable.
For the thousands of small nonprofits operating on shoestring budgets — community
food banks, immigrant services organizations, youth mentorship programs, local
health clinics — it is often impossible.

The result: small nonprofits with the deepest community roots and the most urgent
missions are chronically underfunded, not because their work isn't valuable, but
because they cannot compete with larger organizations that have professional grant
writers on staff.

**This tool levels that playing field.**

## Who This Is For

- A two-person nonprofit that serves 500 families but has no development director
- A grassroots immigrant services organization applying for their first grant
- A community health clinic trying to expand into mental health services
- A youth after-school program trying to become financially sustainable
- A nonprofit executive director who wears ten hats and has no time to write

---

## What It Does Differently

Most grant resources online are either:
1. Generic tips ("write a compelling narrative") — not actionable
2. Expensive grant-writing consultants — out of reach for small orgs
3. Grant databases that list opportunities but don't help you evaluate fit

This tool does three specific things those resources don't:

**1. Personalized matching with reasoning.**
It doesn't just list grants — it explains *why* a specific grant is a good fit
for *your specific organization*, flagging eligibility concerns before you invest
hours applying.

**2. Structured first drafts.**
Starting from a blank page is the hardest part. The tool generates a complete
structured draft — all 10 standard sections — with clear markers where the human
writer needs to insert specific data. This turns a 20-hour task into a 4-hour one.

**3. Accessibility.**
A nonprofit with one staff member and a laptop can use this tool. No consultant
fees, no subscription, no expertise required.

## Limitations and Ethical Notes

This tool is a starting point, not a final product. Users must:

- **Verify all facts** — Claude may occasionally hallucinate statistics or details.
  Every factual claim in the draft must be verified before submission.
- **Personalize the narrative** — A funder can tell when a proposal was generated
  without human voice. The draft needs to be edited to sound like *your* organization.
- **Check grant deadlines and eligibility** — Grant programs change. Always verify
  current requirements directly on the funder's website before applying.
- **Add real data** — The draft uses placeholders ([INSERT: ...]) where specific
  statistics or outcomes data should go. Those must be filled with real numbers.

**This tool does not guarantee funding.** Grant success depends on program quality,
relationship with the funder, timing, and many factors beyond the proposal itself.

---

## Connection to AI Safety and Responsible Use

This project intentionally demonstrates responsible AI deployment:

- The tool is transparent about being AI-generated and requires human review
- It inserts explicit [INSERT: ...] placeholders rather than fabricating data
- The IMPACT.md file acknowledges limitations honestly
- It is designed to *augment* human work, not replace the human judgment
  that grant writing requires

This reflects the AI safety principle that the best AI tools are ones that
keep humans meaningfully in the loop — especially for high-stakes outputs
like grant proposals that organizations depend on for survival.
